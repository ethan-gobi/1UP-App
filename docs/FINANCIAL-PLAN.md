## Year two and ownership

Repeating 12 selling days at the assumed small-event rate would cost an estimated CAD $29,143.84 before tax and contingency. This keeps $15,060 for host and transport, $3,072 for stall shifts, $864 for mechanical inspections, $2,307.84 for software maintenance, $1,140 for software services, $2,000 for insurance and agreement review, $300 for payment fees and $4,400 for coordination. With the same conservative 13% tax allowance and 7% contingency, allow $35,237.82. This is a separate future-year scenario. Product replenishment comes from sales proceeds; only gross margin after product costs can help pay running costs.

A possible payer is Evergreen's Garden Market operating budget, supplemented by an event sponsor or donated venue time. No funding commitment has been made. After four drills and the first trading season, compare actual gross margin and avoided losses with the annual cost. If the gap is large, renegotiate shared space or reduce the number of sessions before renewing.

For the pilot, nominate Ethan as the student app maintenance contact, subject to his acceptance. Before any public launch, Evergreen would also need to appoint a continuing service owner or contracted maintainer with access to the repository, hosting account, backups and handover notes. The paid maintenance allowance covers 24 hours a year. The 160 contributed hours cover a mocked approval, count, reservation and collection flow. Paid checkout, public alerts and a production rollout require separate scope and ownership.
