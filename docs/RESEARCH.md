# Research task 1: Could Copp Hall host our backup Garden Market?

- Find Rosedale United Church on the [TRCA floodplain map](https://trca.ca/conservation/flood-risk-management/flood-plain-map-viewer/). Save a screenshot showing the site, the floodplain boundary and the route customers would use.
- Check the [Copp Hall layout](https://www.rosedaleunited.org/rent-with-us/copp-hall). Trace the cabinet from storage to the sales floor and a customer from the accessible entrance to the counter. Check door widths and lift access.
- Find out whether the church can offer dry, lockable storage for one stocked cabinet year-round. Ask whether water has ever entered the storage room.
- Check whether vendors can sell seeds and books there and how much notice the church needs. Who can unlock the hall on a weekend if Brick Works closes?
- Use the [rental rates page](https://www.rosedaleunited.org/pages/rates) to check costs. Get separate prices for monthly storage, one eight-hour weekend market and any staffing or cleaning fees.
- Finish with one decision: can we offer short-notice pickups, scheduled pop-ups only, or do we need another host? Record the source or written answer behind that decision.
