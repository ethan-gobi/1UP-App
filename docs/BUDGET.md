## Website: $100

## Year-two reserve: $29,783.89

The old plan's biggest weakness was that year two (about $35,238) had no payer. This reserve is held back and released only after the year-one review shows the stall was used and the drills passed.

- At the $100/h hall rate, year two costs about $31,069 (without app spending), so the reserve covers **96%** of it.
- At the $200/h rate, year two costs about $42,676, so the reserve covers **70%**. Evergreen, a sponsor or donated hall time would cover the gap.
- If the year-one review says stop, the reserve is returned unspent.
