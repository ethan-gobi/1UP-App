# Yellow Zone Flood Warning App

The flood warning app for the Evergreen Brick Works Yellow Zone foldable-shelves pilot (1UP).

- Simple plan: [docs/PLAN-SIMPLE.md](docs/PLAN-SIMPLE.md)
- Detailed plan: [docs/PLAN-DETAILED.md](docs/PLAN-DETAILED.md)

## The app

[`app/`](app/README.md) is the Brick Works Flood Desk: a live Don River flood forecast, the daily flood protocol for each person (built around Maya, site operations), and the Fold-Out Market pickup. Static, free to host.

[`model/`](model/README.md) explains how the forecast was built and tested, including the 75% humidity rule.
