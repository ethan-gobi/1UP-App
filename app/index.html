        How good is the forecast?

        Tested on 2020 to 2023, unseen in training
        20 of 20high-water events flagged at Watch or above, a day ahead or on the day
        4.8false-alarm Watch days a year
        15 of 20events reached Warning, with 1.3 false-alarm days a year

        Plain day-by-day accuracy is 99.0%, but a model that never warns scores 98.4% because high water is rare. That is why the event counts above are the honest measure.

        Toronto's humidity passes 75% on about 9 days in 10, flood or not, so the rule warns almost every day. Humidity in the hours before rain does carry a little signal, and the model keeps it as one input. Rain, the rain of the last few days and snowmelt do most of the work.

        Method and limits

        Question. Will the Don at Todmorden (gauge 02HC024) average 20 m³/s or more today? That level was reached on 178 days from 2000 to 2023, about 7 a year. The July 2013 flood averaged 53 m³/s.

        Inputs. Today's rain and the two days before, a 0.85-per-day fading total of past rain (how soaked the ground is), heaviest hourly rain, snowmelt, warmth, peak humidity, humidity in the 5 hours before the heaviest rain, pressure swing and season.

        Data. Rain from Environment Canada stations (Toronto City, North York, Pearson). Other weather from the Open-Meteo archive. River flow from the Water Survey of Canada. All free, no keys.

        Training. Logistic regression trained on 2000 to 2015, alert levels picked on 2016 to 2019, scored once on 2020 to 2023. The shipped version is refit on 2000 to 2023. Retrain with node model/train.mjs.

        After 2023. Official daily flows stop at 2023, so later floods were checked by hand: 16 July 2024 scored 94% (Warning), 17 August 2024 scored 21% (Watch) and 2 September 2026 scored 20% (Watch, borderline).

        Limits. A forecast is only as good as tomorrow's rain forecast, and summer cloudbursts are the hardest to forecast. The model works by day, so it cannot promise a 5-hour lead on its own. Pair it with the live gauge card, and treat Watch as "get ready".
